Tamil Nagaram — how to play
Move: WASD / Arrow keys (desktop) or the joystick (mobile)
Sprint: hold Shift while walking
Interact: press E near a person or place, or tap the round action button
Vehicle: tap 🚶/🛺 to switch between walking and driving the auto-rickshaw
Inventory (I): tap food items to eat them and restore energy
Quests (Q): talk to townsfolk to discover and track quests
Shops: opened automatically during certain conversations — buy with 💰, sell to earn
Gifts: if you're carrying someone's favourite item, a gift option appears in their dialogue
Mini-games: fishing at the harbor, cooking at tea stalls, kolam at the park, panipuri at the cart, paperwork at the government office, and traffic dodging
Day/Night: the clock runs automatically; toggle with 🌙 in the action bar
Fast Travel (🗺️): jump straight to any district you've already visited
Codex (📖): a living reference of every person, place and item in the city
Photo (📷): save a PNG snapshot of the city to your device
Save: pause (⏸ or Esc) and tap சேமி to save — the game also autosaves periodically
